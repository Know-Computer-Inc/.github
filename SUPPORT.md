# Support

Where to go, depending on what you need.

| If you need... | Go here |
|---|---|
| Help with a public Know repository | Open an issue in that repository using its issue templates |
| Report a bug in a public repository | **Bug report** issue form in that repository |
| Propose a feature or an engineering improvement | **Feature proposal** or **Engineering improvement** issue form |
| Fix a typo or a documentation gap | **Documentation issue** issue form |
| Report a security vulnerability | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — **privately, never in an issue** |
| Report a conduct concern | [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) — privately |
| Understand how contributions work | [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) |
| General questions about Know Computer | See below |

## General questions

Know Computer is an early-stage company without a public support desk, a
community forum, or a published contact address. Until one exists, the honest
answer is: watch the [Know-Computer-Inc organization](https://github.com/Know-Computer-Inc).
Product, documentation, and hiring information will be published there as it
becomes real.

Do not send private or sensitive material to public issue trackers in the hope
that someone will move it somewhere safe — it will be public.

## What we do not support

- Questions about private repositories, internal systems, or internal tooling.
  Those are not visible to you, and we will not describe them in public.
- Requests to prioritize, roadmap, or estimate work.
- Security reports delivered through public issues, pull requests, or social
  media. These are closed without discussion and re-routed.

## Response expectations

There is no published response-time commitment. Public repositories are
maintained by a small team alongside product work; issues are triaged when
capacity allows, and security reports are handled ahead of ordinary issues.
