## Context

What is happening, what constraint or force is acting on us, and what made this
a decision rather than a default. Include the evidence: measurements, failure
modes, requirements, links to issues or incidents. Assume the reader knows
nothing about the situation and has ten minutes.

Keep confidential details out of this file if the repository is (or will be)
public: describe the constraint without leaking the customer, the deal, or the
internal system.

## Decision

The choice we made, stated in the active voice, in one or two sentences.
"We will..." or "We choose...". Ambiguity here becomes a dispute later.

## Alternatives considered

Each option seriously evaluated, with why it lost. Include the option of doing
nothing (it was on the table, even if nobody wanted it).
