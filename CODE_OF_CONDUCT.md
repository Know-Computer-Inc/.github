# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Know Computer, Inc. is a small team working on a hard, long-term problem. The
quality of that work depends on how we treat each other.

We expect:

- **Professionalism.** Disagree with ideas directly; do not attack people.
- **Respect.** Assume competence and good faith, including from people you do
  not know and people who are new to the discussion.
- **Precision.** Say what you know, what you infer, and what you do not know.
  Claims should be checkable.
- **Responsibility.** If you cause a problem, say so early and help fix it.
- **Privacy.** Do not share other people's personal information, work, or
  private communications without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated, in code review, issues, pull requests,
discussions, commits, messages, or any space where we communicate:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Deliberately misleading claims, including misrepresenting test results,
  credentials, or other people's work.
- Publishing private information (doxxing) or anything shared in confidence.
- Sexual or romantic advances in professional settings where they are not
  welcome.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or insistence on relitigating settled
  decisions after a maintainer has closed the thread.
- Advocating for, or participating in, security vulnerabilities being disclosed
  publicly or exploited.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider hostile or abusive.

## Reporting a report

**Report privately. Do not open a public issue, pull request, or discussion.**

Report an incident by one of these routes:

1. GitHub's report form for abuse involving a GitHub account:
   <https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>
2. Email **m@know.computer** (Mattia Ciuni, CEO at Know Computer) directly and
   privately.
3. Contact a Know organization administrator directly through GitHub. Profile
   and organization pages list the people with administrator access.

Include: what happened, when and where it happened (link, repository, thread),
who was involved, and any supporting evidence. Preserve screenshots or messages
that may be needed to understand the report.

Reports are handled by a Know organization administrator who is not the subject
of the complaint whenever that is possible. We treat report contents as
confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair,
proportionate action in response to behavior they judge to be inappropriate.

Contributions may be rejected, reverted, locked, or restricted, and people may
be temporarily or permanently removed from the project, depending on severity,
pattern, and context. Where a pattern is unclear, we will ask questions before
deciding.

Appeals go to a different Know organization administrator than the one who made
the decision.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Know Computer project spaces (repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions, and related communication channels) and
whenever someone is representing Know Computer in public.

It does not replace employment, contractor, or legal agreements; where those set
a higher standard, that standard applies.
